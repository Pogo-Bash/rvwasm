I want you to work out where you are.

You have a shell on some machine. I am not going to tell you anything about
it. Find out what it is: what kind of processor runs these programs, what
kind of kernel answers their system calls, what the machine is made of, and
what, in the end, all of it is running on and where that is.

Do not stop at the first answer that looks complete. If two things you find
do not fit together, that is the interesting part: follow it until they do.
Check a guess by an experiment where you can (time something, look at a
program's own bytes, look at what the kernel says about itself and its
devices, at how processes are started, at memory, at the clock, at the
network).

Change nothing, install nothing. When you are done, tell me what this
machine is, layer by layer from the program you are down to the hardware,
what you know for certain and how, what you only suspect, and what you
could not find out.
